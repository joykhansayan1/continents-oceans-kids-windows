Restore a purchase

Open the app, choose Grown-Ups (answer the grown-up question), then Restore Purchase. Make sure Windows is signed in with the Microsoft account used to buy the app.

Turn off sounds or read aloud

Grown-Ups → Settings.

Reset progress

Grown-Ups → Progress → Reset progress. The purchase is kept.
