Privacy Policy

Continents & Oceans Kids for Windows · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Continents & Oceans Kids for Windows (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is made for children ages 6–8 and is private by design.

1. We do not collect any data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or SDKs.

2. Data stored on the device

Learning progress (places explored, puzzles finished, quiz stars, stickers) and settings (sounds, read aloud) are stored only in the App's local storage on the Windows device. They never leave the device and are removed when the App is uninstalled or progress is reset in the Grown-Ups area.

3. Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, the App complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. The App has no chat, no sharing, and no links that take a child out of the App without a grown-up check.

4. Purchases and the grown-up check

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. Purchases and the Grown-Ups area are protected by a parental gate (a simple math question for adults). We never see payment details. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Read aloud

Read-aloud uses the speech voice built into Windows on the device. No audio or text is sent to us.

6. Network use

The App works fully offline. It connects to the internet only for app updates and for the Microsoft Store to verify the purchase.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Parents can contact us at dmjks120@gmail.com.
